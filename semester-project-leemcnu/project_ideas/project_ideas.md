## Project Idea 1: Do College Majors Actually Pay Off?

Choosing a college major is one of the most important decisions students make, and many people want to know how it will affect their future salary. This project will explore how salaries differ across majors and how earnings change from the start of a career to mid-career. The goal is to help prospective students understand the financial outcomes associated with different fields of study.

Dataset:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/wsj/college-salaries

This dataset includes information about many college majors and several salary statistics such as starting median salary, mid-career median salary, and salary percentiles.

Questions I will explore:

- Which college majors have the highest starting salaries?

- Which majors show the largest salary growth from starting salary to mid-career salary?

- How do STEM majors compare to non-STEM majors in terms of salary outcomes?

- How wide is the salary distribution within different majors?

Possible visualizations:

- Bar charts showing the highest paying majors

- Scatter plots comparing starting salary and mid-career salary

- Box plots showing salary distributions by major category

- Slope charts showing salary growth from starting to mid-career

## Project Idea 2: What Factors Are Associated With Higher Life Expectancy?

Life expectancy varies widely across countries, and many factors may influence how long people live. This project will explore how life expectancy relates to factors such as economic conditions, education, and health-related variables. The goal is to better understand which factors appear most strongly associated with longer lifespans.

Dataset:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kumarajarshi/life-expectancy-who

This dataset includes information for many countries over multiple years and contains variables such as life expectancy, GDP, education levels, alcohol consumption, BMI, healthcare expenditure, and adult mortality.

Questions I will explore:

- How strongly is GDP associated with life expectancy?

- Do countries with higher education levels have longer lifespans?

- How has life expectancy changed over time globally?

- Are there noticeable differences in life expectancy between countries?

Possible visualizations:

- Scatter plots comparing GDP and life expectancy

- Line charts showing life expectancy trends over time

- Box plots comparing life expectancy across countries or regions

- Correlation plots showing relationships between different variables
